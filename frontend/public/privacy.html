What we collect: almost nothing.

PayWise AI works without an account. The email and amount you type are checked and then discarded.

  • No account, no advertising cookies, no tracking.
  • Your email and payment details are sent to our server to be checked and are not stored.
  • Server logs keep only the verdict and a timestamp.
  • The payment status and reason code are sent to Google Gemini to be reworded. No email or name is attached.

This is a sandbox hackathon project and has not been legally reviewed.
